# heldout-3: pre-registration (AI1, assisted-2)

Registered before any assisted-2 prompt, verifier or configuration exists, from the frozen specification [`heldout-3-spec.md`](heldout-3-spec.md) (committed first, unchanged since). The machine-readable record, [`heldout-3-preregistration.json`](heldout-3-preregistration.json), pins the specification's SHA-256, the case list, every PDF's and every expected output's SHA-256 and their digest; a test fails if any of them, or either pre-registration file, changes.

## Contents
- 24 cases, 168 fields: 65 found, 3 conflict, 100 unknown. Coverage as specified: entity 10, phrasing 3, layout 2, missing 3, conflict 2, ambiguous 1, adversarial 2, duplicate 1.
- Generated by `corpus/generated.py` (seed 20261002); expected outputs by construction from the generation parameters, checked by tests against the parser's text (every evidence occurs on its page, no text off the page) and against our normalizers and the contract (`apps/api/tests/test_generated_corpus.py`). No extractor wrote them.
- assist-dev (10 cases) is generated by disjoint templates for development only; it is listed in the record but not frozen here.

## Protocol and thresholds
As in the specification: rules-1 measured on heldout-3 after this freeze (`heldout-3-baseline.json`); assisted-2 frozen and hashed (prompt, model, configuration, verifier version) before its first heldout-3 call; measured once, three runs over dev, heldout and heldout-3; heldout-2 never sent.

Primary, worst of three runs, against rules-1 on heldout-3: field accuracy and evidence completeness at least +0.15; abstention and conflict recall not lower; wrong values not above rules-1; wrong party names 0; silent conflicts not higher. Hard gates on every run; budget p95 at most 20 s and mean cost at most USD 0.05 per call.

**Decision rule:** enable assisted-2 only if every hard gate holds on every run, every primary threshold holds on the worst run and the budget holds; otherwise report it as measured and ship this milestone rules-only (no assisted-3).

## Limitations
24 cases (directional evidence, reported with counts); one author wrote the templates and will write assisted-2 (mitigated by the frozen specification, generated ground truth, disjoint assist-dev templates and the freeze order); generated text is more regular than real agreements.
